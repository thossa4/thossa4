### 🌊 About me

I'm a geographer working where **GIS, remote sensing and machine learning** meet **natural hazards**. My research builds GeoAI frameworks for multi-hazard susceptibility mapping and land-use vulnerability in coastal Louisiana, so communities and planners can see risk before it arrives.

- 🎓 Ph.D. Candidate in Geography, Louisiana State University
- 🤝 Open to research collaborations and co-authorship
